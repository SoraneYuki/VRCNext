**Create Instance Modal**

**Improvements**
- The profile status heatmap is now far more accurate. A friend's status is recorded the moment they come online, and status changes that happened while VRCNext was closed are caught up on the next start.

- The **Create Instance** has been updated to the v2 vrcn design.

- The profile status heatmap no longer shows green **Online** time for periods where the actual status was never observed. Such periods are now shown as gray **Unknown** and are excluded from the "Mostly" summary.
